• كل المعالجة تتم على جهازك، ولا يُرفع أي شيء إلى أي خادم

## Full description – English
Followers Check shows who unfollowed you, who doesn't follow you back, and who your new followers are, safely.

🔒 No password, ever
• We never ask for your password and never sign in to your account
• Uses the official data file you export yourself from Instagram (JSON format)
• Everything is processed on your device; nothing is uploaded anywhere

📊 Three clear lists
• Unfollowed you: followed you last time, not anymore
• Not following back: accounts you follow that don't follow you
• New followers: who started following you since last time
• Fast search, counters, and one tap to open a profile

📈 Track your growth
• History of every import with follower counts
• Follower chart over time (Premium)

⏰ Smart reminders
• Weekly or bi-weekly reminder to request a fresh export

🌙 Arabic & English, light and dark themes.

⭐ Premium: unlimited comparisons, full history and charts.

Private by design: no accounts, no data collection, no ads, no trackers.

*Followers Check is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Instagram or Meta. Instagram is a trademark of Instagram, LLC.*
