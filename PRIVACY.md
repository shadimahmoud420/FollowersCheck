# Privacy Policy – Followers Check

_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

Followers Check ("the app") is an independent app that helps you compare two
copies of **your own** "Followers and following" data export. It is **not
affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Instagram or Meta**.

## Summary

**All data stays on your device.** The app has no backend, no accounts, no
analytics, no ads and no trackers.

## What the app never does

- It **never asks for your Instagram password** and never signs in to any
  account on your behalf.
- It **never uses any unofficial or private Instagram API** and never scrapes
  websites.
- It **never uploads** your files or any data derived from them.

## What the app processes

| Data | Where it comes from | Where it is stored | Why |
|------|---------------------|--------------------|-----|
| Usernames of your followers and the accounts you follow | The export file(s) **you** choose with the system file picker | A private SQLite database inside the app's sandbox | To compare snapshots |
| Date of each import | Your device clock | Same local database | To order snapshots and draw the history chart |
| Preferences (language, theme, reminder frequency, onboarding completed) | Your choices in the app | Local app preferences | To remember your settings |
| Premium status | The App Store / Google Play purchase flow (when enabled) | Local app preferences | To unlock premium features |

The original export file is read in memory and is not copied or kept by the app.
Other data contained in an export (messages, profile info, etc.) is ignored.

## Network

The app does not request the Android `INTERNET` permission and does not
contact any server. When you tap "Open profile", the public profile URL is
handed to your browser or to another installed app, which then operates under
its own privacy policy. In-app purchases (when enabled) are handled entirely by
Apple / Google.

## Notifications

Optional reminders are **local notifications** scheduled on your device. No
push service is used.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings › Delete all data** permanently deletes all snapshots.
- You can delete a single snapshot from the History screen.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
any personal data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app
update and on this page.
